:::tip[In short]
In Sweden, **antenatal visits** (at the *barnmorskemottagning*, about **6–10 visits**) and your child's check-ups at the **BVC** are **free**. Childhood vaccinations are **free and voluntary**. You have the right to **full parental leave until your child is 18 months old**. **Föräldrapenning** (the parental benefit) covers **480 days** per child between the two parents, and **90 days are reserved for each parent**. If your child is sick (under 12), there is **VAB**. Note: **from 1 January 2027** new requirements apply to some benefits, including föräldrapenning at the basic and minimum level.
:::

## During pregnancy: the barnmorskemottagning

Antenatal care is at the **barnmorskemottagning** (often shortened to MVC), the midwife clinic. The visits are **free** and there are **about 6–10** in total.

**You choose the clinic yourself** and contact it **as soon as you know you are pregnant**. You can usually find it on 1177.se or just phone.

:::cost[Giving birth]
A stay in hospital for the birth carries a **small daily fee that depends on the region**. In Stockholm, for example, a hospital stay costs **130 kr per day** for adults. Check the fee in your own region on 1177.se.
:::

For how healthcare works in general (1177, vårdcentral, registration), see the guide [Healthcare](/en/guide/vard).

## After the birth: the BVC

The **BVC** (*barnavårdscentral*, child health centre) follows your child's development **from birth through förskoleklass**. It is **free**. In the **first year** there are **at least 13 visits**.

The **vaccinations** in the child vaccination programme are **free and voluntary**. They protect against **12 diseases** and start **at 6 weeks** of age.

## Parental leave: your right to stay home

Leave is governed by the Parental Leave Act (*föräldraledighetslagen*, 1995:584). It is not the same thing as the benefit: leave is the **right to be away from work**, föräldrapenning is the **money** you may receive during the leave.

- **Full leave until the child is 18 months old**, **even without** föräldrapenning.
- **Reduced working hours by a quarter (25 %)** until the child is **8** (or has finished the first school year).
- **From 1 July 2028** the limit moves to the **end of the second school year**.

## Föräldrapenning: the numbers

Föräldrapenning is paid by Försäkringskassan. For registration and general rules, see the guide [Försäkringskassan](/en/guide/forsakringskassan).

| | |
|---|---|
| **Total days** | **480** per child between the two parents (**660** for twins) |
| **At the income-related level** | **390** days, **250 to 1,259 kr** per day |
| **At the minimum level** | **90** days, **180 kr** per day |
| **Requirement for the income-related level** | income qualifying for sickness benefit (SGI) of at least **7,083 kr a month for at least 240 days** before the expected birth; otherwise you receive **250 kr** per day |

### Reserved days, double days and transferring days

- **90 reserved days** for each parent, at sickness-benefit level, **cannot be transferred**.
- **Double days (*dubbeldagar*):** days when **both parents are at home together**. There are **60 per child**, up to the child's **15th month**.
- **Transferring days:** since **1 July 2024** you can transfer days to someone else insured in Sweden, for example a **grandparent**. The limit is **45 days** per parent with joint custody and **90** with sole custody. Reserved days cannot be transferred.

:::note[No advance notice]
Since **1 July 2026** you no longer have to notify Försäkringskassan in advance before applying for föräldrapenning.
:::

## VAB: when your child is sick

**VAB** (*vård av barn*) is the benefit for staying home with a **sick child under 12**.

- Up to **120 days a year per child**, shared between the parents.
- The benefit is **a little less than 80 percent** of your income (SGI), with a ceiling.
- Apply **within 30 days** of the first day you were home: through the e-service, the app or a paper form.

:::warn[Keep to the 30 days]
The application must be made **within 30 days** of the first day at home. If you apply later you may lose the benefit for those days.
:::

## Pregnancy benefit (graviditetspenning)

**Graviditetspenning** is for people whose job is **physically heavy or hazardous** and who **cannot be moved** to other tasks. It is about **80 percent of your pay**, **at most 944 kr per day**, from **at most 60 days before the birth** up to the **11th day before**.

## Child allowance (barnbidrag)

**Barnbidrag** is **1,250 kr a month per child**, paid automatically if you live in Sweden with your children, generally until age 16. Details, the large-family supplement and how it is split between two parents are in the guide [Försäkringskassan](/en/guide/forsakringskassan).

| What | Number |

## The qualification rule from 1 January 2027

:::warn[New requirements for some benefits]
The Riksdag approved on **20 May 2026** a new rule that applies **from 1 January 2027**. For some residence-based benefits you will need to have **lived in Sweden for 5 of the last 15 years**, or live in Sweden with **employment income at a certain level for a certain period**. There are exceptions for "exceptional reasons". The benefits affected include **föräldrapenning at the basic and minimum level**, **barnbidrag** and **bostadsbidrag**. A requirement of **lawful stay** is also introduced for being counted as resident.

**If you settled in Sweden before 1 January 2027, the old rules apply to you.** We do not state the income thresholds for the work route: check with Försäkringskassan.
:::

## Children born in Sweden: citizenship

A child is **Swedish from birth if a parent is a Swedish citizen**, wherever the child is born. Place of birth alone does not decide it. The full picture is in the guide [Swedish citizenship](/en/guide/medborgarskap).

## If you are an Italian citizen

If a parent is **Italian**, the birth must be **transcribed** in Italy through the Embassy, and for that the parent must first be **registered with AIRE**. Whether a child born in Sweden is also entitled to Italian citizenship depends on Italian rules: ask the Embassy. See the guide [AIRE and the consulate](/en/guide/aire).

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting to contact the barnmorskemottagning.** Call as soon as you know you are pregnant.
- **Mixing up leave and benefit.** Leave is the right to stay home, föräldrapenning is the money.
- **Thinking the 90 reserved days can be transferred.** They cannot.
- **Forgetting the 30-day limit for VAB.**
- **Assuming the 2027 rule hits those already here.** If you settled before 1 January 2027 the old rules apply, but check your own situation.
- **Not registering the birth in Italy** if you are an Italian citizen: AIRE comes first.
- **Treating Stockholm's fees as if they applied everywhere.** Healthcare fees depend on the region.
