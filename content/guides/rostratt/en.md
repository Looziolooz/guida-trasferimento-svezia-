:::tip[In short]
In Sweden **not everyone votes in everything**. In the **Riksdag** election **only Swedish citizens** can vote. In **municipal and regional** elections **EU, Icelandic and Norwegian citizens** can also vote if they are **registered (*folkbokförda*)**, and **other foreign citizens after 3 consecutive years** of registration. The September 2026 elections have already taken place: **the next ones are in 2030**. Meanwhile, you can often keep voting in your own country.
:::

## The election calendar

The general elections on **13 September 2026** (Riksdag, region and municipality) have already been held. The next general elections are in **2030** (the Election Authority's table gives **8 September 2030**). The next **European Parliament elections** are in **2029**.

So if you have just arrived, **you have not missed an imminent vote**: you have time to get things in order.

## Who can vote where

In every case you must be **18 on election day**.

| Election | Who can vote |
|---|---|
| **Riksdag** | **Swedish citizens** only |
| **Municipality and region** | Swedish citizens; **EU, Icelandic and Norwegian citizens who are *folkbokförda***; **other foreign citizens and stateless people who have been *folkbokförda* for 3 consecutive years** before election day |
| **European Parliament** | Registered EU citizens choose where to vote (see below) |

Being *folkbokförd* means being registered in Sweden: the steps are in the guide [Folkbokföring and personnummer](/en/guide/folkbokforing). **Without registration you are not on the electoral roll.**

:::note[A rejected motion]
In 2026 the Riksdag **rejected** a motion to limit municipal voting to Swedish citizens (report 2025/26:KU27). The rules for municipality and region are therefore unchanged.
:::

## European elections: one country only

If you are an EU citizen and *folkbokförd* in Sweden, you **choose** whether to vote in Sweden or in your home country in the European elections. But **in one country only**.

To vote in Sweden, fill in the form **"Anmälan till den svenska röstlängden för val till Europaparlamentet"** and send it to the **länsstyrelse** (county administrative board), **at least 30 days before** the election.

:::warn[The deadline is early]
The 30 days count back from election day. If you want to vote in Sweden, send the form well ahead of time.
:::

## Voting in the Riksdag: Swedish citizenship

To vote in the Riksdag election you must be a **Swedish citizen**. The route, the requirements and the rules in force since 6 June 2026 are in the guide [Swedish citizenship](/en/guide/medborgarskap). The right to vote in municipal and regional elections, on the other hand, does not require citizenship if you fall into one of the groups in the table.

## Voting in your home country

You can often keep voting in the country whose citizenship you hold, even while living in Sweden. The rules are set by **that country**: check its government or embassy website. As an example, here is **Italy**.

### Example: Italy

- **Register with AIRE.** Voting from abroad goes through registration in the register of Italians resident abroad. How to do it is in the guide [AIRE and the Embassy](/en/guide/aire).
- **General elections and referendums:** people registered with AIRE normally vote by post from abroad, and can choose to vote in Italy by notifying the authorities within the deadlines. The arrangements and deadlines change at each election: **check the Italian Embassy in Stockholm's website** every time.
- **COMITES 2026:** for the elections of the committees of Italians abroad, the Embassy gives **4 November 2026** as the deadline to **register on the list**. Details are in the Embassy's news item.

:::who[Who to contact]
**Swedish elections, electoral roll** → Valmyndigheten (val.se).
**European vote in Sweden** → the länsstyrelse of your county.
**Vote in your home country** → that country's embassy or consulate. For Italy, the Embassy in Stockholm.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming everyone living in Sweden votes in everything.** The Riksdag election requires Swedish citizenship.
- **Not registering (folkbokföring).** Without it you are not on the roll for municipality and region.
- **Forgetting the 3 years** if you are not an EU, Icelandic or Norwegian citizen: 3 consecutive years before election day are required.
- **Voting in two countries in the European elections.** You pick one country.
- **Sending the form to the länsstyrelse too late.** The deadline is at least 30 days before the election.
- **Assuming your home country's rules.** They change at each election: check the official website.
- **For Italians: forgetting AIRE.** Without registration, voting from abroad gets harder, and COMITES also has a deadline (4 November 2026).
