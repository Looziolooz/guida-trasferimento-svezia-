:::tip[In short]
Housing is the hardest part of moving to a big Swedish city. The **housing queues (bostadskö)** mean **years of waiting**, so most newcomers start with a **sublet (andrahandskontrakt)** or a rental from a private owner. **Never pay anything in advance** before you have seen the home and the contract is signed: that is the first rule against scams.
:::

**Want to buy instead of rent?** → [buying a home](/en/guide/kopa-bostad)

## The kinds of contract you will meet

Most rentals in Sweden are a **hyresrätt**: a rental apartment, usually in a building owned by a landlord who rents out many homes. Do not mix it up with a **bostadsrätt**, where someone *owns a share* in a housing association and has the right to live in the apartment. This guide is only about renting.

| Type | What it is | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Förstahandskontrakt** (first-hand) | A contract directly with the landlord of a hyresrätt. | Usually reached through the housing queues. The most secure, but with the longest waits in big cities. |
| **Andrahandskontrakt** (second-hand) | A tenant sublets their hyresrätt to you. | The most common way to start. The sublet must be approved by the landlord or by the Hyresnämnden. Fixed rules apply to the rent (see below). |
| **Renting from a private owner** | You rent a house, a bostadsrätt, an owned apartment or a room from a private individual. | A dedicated law applies from 1 July 2026 (see below). |
| **A room in someone's home** | You rent a room in the home of the owner or another tenant. | Counts as renting from a private person. Agree kitchen, bathroom, guests and costs in writing. |

The **Hyresnämnden** (the rent tribunal) settles disputes between tenants and landlords about rent. You will see it mentioned several times here.

## Housing queues (bostadskö)

Many public and private landlords hand out first-hand apartments through a **bostadskö**, a queue you join online. Joining costs a **small yearly fee**, and what counts is **how long you have been registered**, not how urgently you need a home.

In big cities the wait is **years**. The practical rule is: **sign up now**, even if you think you will stay a short time, and before you have any apartment in mind. Every year without a registration is a year lost. Check each queue's website for its conditions and for how to keep your registration active: if you do not renew it, you may lose your place.

:::note[While you wait]
The queue does not rule out other routes. Many people sublet or rent from a private owner for the first years, and collect queue time in the meantime.
:::

## The new private-rental law (from 1 July 2026)

From **1 July 2026** a new law on private rentals (*privatuthyrningslag*, SFS 2026:772) applies when you rent a house, a bostadsrätt, an owned apartment or a room from a private person. In short:

- **The rent must be reasonable compared with similar homes.** If it looks too high, you can turn to the **Hyresnämnden**.
- **3 months' notice** for you as the tenant if you want to leave.
- **No right to renewal:** the landlord can choose not to renew the contract when it ends.

The last point matters: a private contract gives you less security than a first-hand contract. If you have a family or a job that depends on the home, think about it before signing and ask for the length and the conditions to be written into the contract.

## Subletting a hyresrätt: the rent rules

When you sublet a hyresrätt, the tenant who sublets may ask at most for a **reasonable rent**, starting from their own. According to the Swedish courts (domstol.se):

- if the home is **furnished**, the tenant may add **at most 15 %**;
- **extra costs** (electricity, internet, parking) are charged **at actual cost**, with no mark-up;
- if you paid too much, you can ask the **Hyresnämnden** to get the **excess back for up to 2 years** (for contracts made after 1 October 2019);
- you must do this **within 3 months of the end of the contract**: after that the right is lost.

:::warn[Ask what the tenant pays]
Before signing, ask what the tenant pays their own landlord and ask to see the first-hand contract. Without that figure you cannot know whether you are being charged more than the rules allow.
:::

## Deposits and a written contract

A deposit is common. **No legal maximum has been verified**, so do not trust figures you hear on the street: have the amount written in the contract, get a receipt, and agree in writing how and when it will be returned. A very large deposit demanded before you have a contract is a warning sign (see the scam section).

Always insist on a **written contract** with the owner's name, address, rent, length, what is included and the notice period. A verbal agreement leaves you with nothing to stand on if something goes wrong.

## How to recognise rental scams

:::danger[Rental scams are common]
The key rule from the Swedish Police is: **"Betala aldrig i förskott innan du har fått se lägenheten och kontraktet är påskrivet"**, which means *never pay in advance before you have seen the apartment and the contract is signed.*

Signs of a scam:
- they ask for money **before they meet you** or show you the home;
- the landlord is **"abroad"** and asks for a deposit to post you the keys;
- they ask you to pay through **anonymous services**;
- there is **no written contract**.

What to do: check that the person subletting is **really the tenant** and has permission to sublet, **ask for an ID document**, and always get a written contract. If you have been scammed: **call your bank first**, then report it to the police on **114 14**.
:::

So the first viewing should be **in person**, or at least a video call where the owner really shows you the home. A home that looks too good and too cheap is almost always a trap.

## Before and after the move

- **Registration:** once you have an address you need it for [folkbokföring](/en/guide/folkbokforing) if you stay at least a year.
- **For non-EU citizens** the residence permit is another step to handle in parallel: [uppehållstillstånd](/en/guide/uppehallstillstand).
- To open an account and use online services you will need [BankID](/en/guide/bankid): many banks ask for a personnummer.

:::who[Who to contact]
**Rent disputes and a rent that is too high** → the Hyresnämnden.
**Information on your rights as a tenant** → Konsumentverket and domstol.se.
**If you are scammed** → your bank, then the police (114 14).
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Paying before seeing the home or signing.** The most frequent case, and the hardest to recover from.
- **Joining the queue late.** Time is what counts: the later you sign up, the longer the wait.
- **Accepting a sublet rent without knowing what the tenant pays.** Remember the 15 % limit for furnished homes.
- **Not getting the landlord's approval** when subletting: without it you risk being asked to leave.
- **Forgetting the 3 months.** To reclaim excess rent you have three months from the end of the contract.
- **Signing a private contract without reading the length.** There is no right to renewal.
