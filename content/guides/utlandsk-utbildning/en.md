:::tip[In short]
You **do not need any recognition to apply for jobs in Sweden**. Your foreign qualification is assessed by **UHR** (the Swedish Council for Higher Education): the assessment is **free**, you apply online, and it takes on average **2–4 months**. But it is **not a Swedish degree and does not replace a licence**. If your profession is **regulated** (for example doctor, nurse, teacher or lawyer), you must obtain a **licence** (*legitimation*) from the competent authority, and that is what decides whether you may practise.
:::

## Two different things: assessment and licence

People arriving with a foreign qualification often mix up two procedures:

- **Assessment of your education (UHR):** a document stating **what your qualification corresponds to in Sweden**. It gives no professional rights.
- **Licence (*legitimation*):** the authorisation to practise a regulated profession. It is issued by the authority for that profession, not by UHR.

Most jobs (office work, technical roles, retail, restaurants) have no licence at all: you only need to convince the employer.

## The UHR assessment

UHR tells you what your qualification corresponds to in Sweden: **upper secondary school** (*gymnasium*), **post-secondary vocational education**, or **university**.

- **Cost:** the document is **free**.
- **How:** through the **online** application on UHR's website.
- **Time:** on average **2–4 months** from when the application is complete.

### Who can apply

You need to meet one of these conditions:

- you have a **residence permit or right of residence in Sweden** and a **personnummer**;
- you are an **EU/EEA citizen**;
- you are **from outside the EU** and have a permit or **family ties to an EU citizen**.

For permits and right of residence, see the guide [Residence permits and right of residence](/en/guide/uppehallstillstand).

:::warn[What the assessment does NOT do]
- It is **not a Swedish degree**: it only states the level your qualification corresponds to.
- You **do not need it to look for work**.
- It **does not replace a licence** (*legitimation*): for regulated professions another authority decides, as explained below.
:::

## Regulated professions: who decides and in which language

In a regulated profession you cannot practise with your foreign qualification alone. The table summarises the cases covered in this guide.

| Profession | Who decides | Language |
|---|---|---|
| Doctor, dentist, pharmacist, nurse, midwife (EU/EEA qualification) | Socialstyrelsen, **automatic** recognition | Swedish (or Danish/Norwegian); accepted proof includes **level C1** |
| Other regulated health professions (for example physiotherapist, psychologist, speech therapist, dental hygienist, assistant nurse) | Socialstyrelsen, through an **assessment** | Swedish (or Danish/Norwegian); **C1** accepted |
| Health profession with a non-EU/EEA qualification (for example doctor) | Socialstyrelsen, 5-step route | Swedish (or Danish/Norwegian) |
| Teacher | Skolverket (*lärarlegitimation*) | university-level Swedish (for example **TISUS** or **C1**) |
| Lawyer qualified in another EU country | Advokatsamfundet (registration as *EU-advokat*) | not stated here: ask the Bar |

:::note[Other professions]
This guide covers health, teaching and law. For any other regulated profession, ask the authority for your field before you move or sign a contract.
:::

## Health professions: Socialstyrelsen

For health professions the authority is **Socialstyrelsen**, which issues the **licence** (*legitimation*).

### EU/EEA qualification

- **Doctors, dentists, pharmacists, nurses and midwives** with an EU/EEA qualification get **automatic** recognition.
- For **other regulated professions** (physiotherapist, psychologist, speech therapist, dental hygienist, assistant nurse and others) your qualification goes through an **assessment**.
- **Language:** you need **Swedish** (or Danish or Norwegian). Accepted proof includes **level C1**.
- **Time:** Socialstyrelsen starts processing **within 4 weeks** and decides **within 3–4 months** of a complete application.

We do not list fees: the sources we found conflict. Check the current amount on Socialstyrelsen's website before you pay.

### Non-EU/EEA qualification (example: doctor)

The route has **5 steps**:

1. assessment of the qualification;
2. proficiency test;
3. course on Swedish laws and rules;
4. clinical placement;
5. licence application.

It usually takes **2–4 years**. An alternative is a **supplementary programme at a university**, taking **1–3 years**, with **limited places**.

:::tip
While you work on the language you can take Swedish classes: see [SFI, work and a-kassa](/en/guide/sfi-och-arbete). C1 is an accepted language proof.
:::

## Teachers: Skolverket

To teach in Swedish schools with a foreign degree you apply for a ***lärarlegitimation*** from **Skolverket**.

- Your education must correspond to the Swedish one **to a sufficient extent**.
- You need **university-level Swedish**, for example **TISUS** or **C1**. Native speakers of Nordic languages are exempt.
- **Cost:** **1 890 kr**.
- **How:** online with **BankID** or **Freja eID+**, or **by post**. For BankID see the guide [BankID, bank account and ID card](/en/guide/bankid).

## Lawyers

If you are qualified in **another EU country** and work in Sweden on a lasting basis, you register as an ***EU-advokat*** with **Advokatsamfundet**, the Swedish Bar Association. Conditions and documents are on the official registration page.

## Skills without a certificate: validering

Do you have experience but no certificate, or a qualification that is not recognised? **Arbetsförmedlingen** offers ***validering***: an assessment of skills that do not show up in a diploma. Ask about it when you register as a jobseeker: more in the guide [SFI, work and a-kassa](/en/guide/sfi-och-arbete).

## Unions and collective agreements

For pay, employment contracts and unemployment insurance, see the guide [Unions and unemployment funds](/en/guide/fack-och-a-kassa).

:::who[Who to contact]
**Assessment of your education** → UHR.
**Health professions** → Socialstyrelsen.
**Teachers** → Skolverket.
**EU lawyers** → Advokatsamfundet.
**Validering and job search** → Arbetsförmedlingen.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Thinking the UHR assessment qualifies you to practise.** It is not a Swedish degree and does not replace a *legitimation*.
- **Waiting for the assessment before applying for jobs.** You do not need it to look for work.
- **Starting work in a regulated profession without a licence.** Ask the competent authority first.
- **Underestimating the language requirement.** Health and teaching need Swedish at a high level (C1 is accepted proof).
- **Miscounting the time.** UHR 2–4 months, Socialstyrelsen 3–4 months, and for a non-EU doctor usually 2–4 years.
- **Not checking Socialstyrelsen's fees** on the official website.
- **Forgetting BankID** if you want to apply online to Skolverket (otherwise you can send it by post).
- **Not using validering** when your skills have no certificate behind them.
