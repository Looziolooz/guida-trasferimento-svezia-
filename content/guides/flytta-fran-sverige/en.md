:::tip[In short]
If you plan to live abroad **for a year or more**, you must **notify Skatteverket**. After that come Försäkringskassan, Pensionsmyndigheten and CSN, if you deal with them. Two things to watch: if you are a **Swedish citizen** or have lived here **at least 10 years**, you can still be taxed in Sweden for **5 years** after you leave, and residence-based benefits end when you move. This guide is a checklist, with a numbered "before you go" list at the end.
:::

## Notify Skatteverket

This is where you start. Skatteverket says:

> *«Om du planerar att bo utomlands ett år eller mer ska du anmäla detta till Skatteverket.»*
> (If you plan to live abroad for a year or more, you must notify Skatteverket.)

You do it through Skatteverket's **e-service** or on **form SKV 7665**. We have not verified a deadline in days: do it **before you leave or right after**, and don't wait for months. Keep the confirmation.

For population registration in general, see the guide [Folkbokföring](/en/guide/folkbokforing).

## The 5-year rule: väsentlig anknytning

:::warn[Leaving does not automatically end Swedish tax]
If you are a **Swedish citizen** or have lived in Sweden **at least 10 years**, then for **5 years** after you leave **you** must show that you no longer have an **essential connection** (*väsentlig anknytning*) to Sweden. If you can't, **you stay taxable in Sweden**. Examples of connections: a **home you can live in all year**, **family**, a **business** or **property** in Sweden.
:::

In practice: before you go, list which of these ties you have and decide what to do with them (sell, let, close). If in doubt, ask Skatteverket or a tax adviser. For the basics of Swedish tax, see [Tax and tax return](/en/guide/skatt-och-deklaration).

## SINK: tax on Swedish income after you leave

After you leave, **Swedish income** such as **salary** and **pension** is taxed under **SINK** (*särskild inkomstskatt för utomlandsbosatta*, special income tax for non-residents):

| Year | SINK rate |
|---|---|
| 2026 | **22.5 %** (previously 25 %) |
| From 1 January 2027 | **20 %** |

- On **pensions** there is an allowance of **3,799 kr per month** in 2026.
- With a **SINK decision** you don't file a tax return.
- You apply through the e-service (an email is enough) or on **form SKV 4350**, **every year**.

:::note[Italy–Sweden]
There is a double taxation treaty between Italy and Sweden (signed in Rome on 6 March 1980, Italian law 439/1982). Which country taxes what **depends on the type of income**: the treaty decides. Get advice from a tax adviser.
:::

## Försäkringskassan

*«Du ska anmäla till Försäkringskassan när du flyttar till ett annat land.»* (You must notify Försäkringskassan when you move to another country.) Do it on **Mina sidor**, on **form 5459** or by phone on **0771-524 524**.

When you move abroad you are **probably no longer insured in Sweden**, so benefits such as child allowance or parental benefit may end. Ask Försäkringskassan what applies to you before you leave. Overview: [Försäkringskassan](/en/guide/forsakringskassan).

## Pensionsmyndigheten

- Report your **foreign address**.
- Send in a **levnadsintyg** (certificate of life) every year.
- **Garantipension** is based on residence and is paid **only to people living in Sweden**.
- **Inkomstpension, premiepension and tilläggspension** you keep in any country.
- Pension paid to someone living abroad is subject to SINK (see above).

More in the guide [Pension](/en/guide/pension).

## Job seeking abroad while on unemployment benefit: the U2

With the **U2** certificate you can look for work in another **EU/EEA country**, in **Switzerland** (or in the **United Kingdom**, under the withdrawal agreement) for **up to 3 months** and keep your Swedish benefit.

:::danger[Apply before you leave]
Request the U2 **before you leave Sweden**. Conditions: you are **entitled to benefit**, **unemployed**, **registered with Arbetsförmedlingen** and **available for work for at least 4 weeks** beforehand.
:::

On a-kassa in general: [Union and a-kassa](/en/guide/fack-och-a-kassa).

## CSN

If you have a student loan, tell **CSN** your new address. To pay from abroad, **register the foreign address first**.

## Your car: deregistering for export

If you take your car out of Sweden you **deregister it for export** (*avregistrering*), with **proof that the car has left the country**. **Receipts and invoices are not enough.** Until then you keep paying **vehicle tax** and insurance. More on cars and licences: [Driving licence and car](/en/guide/korkort-och-bil).

## If you are Italian and moving back to Italy

You take up residence in Italy again by **registering at the registry office (anagrafe) of your Italian comune**. The comune is also the only body that **removes you from AIRE**, so there is no separate step in Sweden. How AIRE works: [AIRE and the consulate](/en/guide/aire). For the Italian side (residence, documents) there is the sister site: [Italia Pratica, residence guide](https://italia-pratica.it/en/guide/residenza).

## Before you go: the list

1. **Notify Skatteverket of the move** (e-service or SKV 7665).
2. **Review your ties to Sweden** if you are a Swedish citizen or have lived here 10 years or more (home, family, business).
3. **Tell Försäkringskassan** (Mina sidor, form 5459 or 0771-524 524).
4. **Write to Pensionsmyndigheten** with your new address and get ready for the *levnadsintyg*.
5. **If you want to job-seek abroad on benefit**, request the **U2** before you leave.
6. **Tell CSN** and register the foreign address.
7. **Deregister the car for export** (proof it left the country).
8. **Union and a-kassa**: cancel or ask what happens to your membership ([guide](/en/guide/fack-och-a-kassa)).
9. **If you are Italian**: register with your Italian comune, which also handles AIRE.

## Common mistakes

- **Not notifying Skatteverket.** It is step one and cannot be skipped.
- **Assuming Swedish tax stops the day you leave**, if you are a Swedish citizen or have lived here 10 years or more: for 5 years you must show you have no essential connection.
- **Not requesting the U2 before you leave.** Afterwards it is too late.
- **Forgetting the levnadsintyg.** It has to be sent in every year.
- **Counting on garantipension abroad.** It is paid only to people living in Sweden.
- **Forgetting the car.** Receipts don't count: you need proof of export, and until it is deregistered you pay tax and insurance.
- **Forgetting CSN and Försäkringskassan.** Each needs its own notice.
