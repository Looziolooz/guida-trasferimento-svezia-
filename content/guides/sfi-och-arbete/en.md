:::tip[In short]
The Swedish course for adults is called **SFI** (*svenska för invandrare*, Swedish for immigrants). You are entitled to it from the second half of the year you turn 16, and **no personnummer is required**. From 1 January 2026 there is a **3-year limit**. For work, the public agency is **Arbetsförmedlingen**. To be protected if you lose your job you need the **a-kassa**: **join early**, because the level of the benefit depends on how long you have been a member.
:::

## SFI: the Swedish course for adults

**SFI** is the public Swedish course for adults who lack the basics of the language. It is run by the **municipality (kommun)** where you live, and that is where you apply.

### Who is entitled

According to Skolverket (the Swedish National Agency for Education), you are entitled to SFI **from the second half of the year you turn 16**, if you live in Sweden and lack the basics of Swedish. Key points:

- **A personnummer is not required**, and the municipality may not demand one. You can start before you are registered.
- **EU/EEA and Swiss citizens** are treated as residents even if they are not yet *folkbokförd* (registered in the Swedish population register). For registration see the guide on [folkbokföring](/en/guide/folkbokforing).
- **Asylum seekers** and **people staying irregularly** are not entitled to SFI.
- **People who already speak enough Danish or Norwegian** are not entitled.

:::note[Free by law]
SFI is **free of charge**: the Education Act (skollagen, chapter 20, section 7) says municipal adult education *ska vara avgiftsfri* — must be free — and textbooks and other learning materials are provided at no cost. At most, the municipality can ask you to bring a few personal learning tools of your own.
:::

### The 3-year limit from 1 January 2026

From **1 January 2026** the right to SFI lasts **3 years from first admission**. For people already enrolled before, the clock counts from 1 January 2026. It can be extended **by 6 months at a time** for particular reasons, such as parental leave, illness or work, but **never beyond 3 years in total**.

In practice: **the time runs from the moment you start**. If you have a choice, begin when you can really follow the course regularly, because the years do not "freeze" if you skip lessons, unless an extension for the listed reasons applies.

### How to apply

Contact the **municipality** (kommun) where you live: the municipal website usually has a page called "SFI" or "svenska för invandrare". You will give your details and your starting level. The practical arrangements (enrolment, locations, schedules, initial test) differ from one municipality to another: check your own municipality's website.

## Arbetsförmedlingen: the public employment service

**Arbetsförmedlingen** is the public employment agency. You register as a **jobseeker** (*arbetssökande*), and registration is also needed to claim the a-kassa. The agency's website has pages in other languages, English among them.

Before you write your CV, remember that the rules for working depend on where you come from:

- **EU/EEA citizens:** no permit is needed to work. See the guide on [uppehållstillstånd](/en/guide/uppehallstillstand) for residence rights.
- **Non-EU citizens:** you normally need a work-related permit, and the employer must meet the pay and contract conditions. This too is in the guide on [uppehållstillstånd](/en/guide/uppehallstillstand).

For details on concrete services (interviews, programmes, courses), the reference is Arbetsförmedlingen's own website: the conditions change and we do not repeat them here.

## A-kassa: unemployment insurance

The **a-kassa** (*arbetslöshetskassa*) is unemployment insurance. From **1 October 2025** a **new, income-based system** applies.

A union and an a-kassa are two separate memberships, and the benefit level depends on how long you have been a member: see [union and a-kassa](/en/guide/fack-och-a-kassa).

### The conditions in brief

The main rule requires:

- at least **120,000 kr of income** in the reference period;
- at least **11,000 kr a month in at least 4 months**;
- a maximum monthly income taken into account of **34,000 kr**;
- being **at least 20 years old**.

### The benefit level depends on membership

| If you have been a member of an a-kassa... | Level |
|---|---|
| ...for the whole reference period | **80 %** |
| ...at least for the last 6 months | **60 %** |
| ...not a member, or a member for a shorter time | **50 %** |

The point to understand: **the gap between 80 % and 50 % is the share of your income that will be covered.** Not joining, or joining at the last moment, is expensive.

:::warn[Why you should join early]
Under the new rules, someone who is **not a member** (or has been one only briefly) gets just the **50 %** level. To reach **60 %** you need to have been a member for at least the last 6 months; for **80 %**, for the whole reference period. If you have just arrived and have a job, **join from the first month**: every month counts, and joining after you lose your job is too late for the higher levels.
:::

The reference period and the other calculation rules are set by the authorities: for the details and the exact calculation in your case, see the pages of Arbetsförmedlingen and the IAF (the inspectorate that supervises the funds), listed below.

## Getting it all in order, in the right order

1. **Registration and number:** if you stay at least a year, [folkbokföring](/en/guide/folkbokforing); if less than a year, the [samordningsnummer](/en/guide/samordningsnummer).
2. **SFI:** apply to the municipality; no personnummer needed.
3. **Arbetsförmedlingen:** register as a jobseeker.
4. **A-kassa:** join as soon as you start working, to move up a level over time.
5. **BankID:** to use online services, see [BankID](/en/guide/bankid).

:::who[Who to contact]
**SFI course** → the municipality (kommun) where you live.
**Work and registration as a jobseeker** → Arbetsförmedlingen.
**Questions about the a-kassa rules** → your fund and the IAF pages.
**Residence-based benefits (allowances, leave)** → see [Försäkringskassan](/en/guide/forsakringskassan).
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting to join the a-kassa.** The level of the benefit depends on how long you have been a member.
- **Believing SFI requires a personnummer.** It does not, and the municipality cannot ask for one.
- **Ignoring the 3-year limit.** From 1 January 2026 the right expires; extensions are 6 months for specific reasons.
- **Paying for a private Swedish course before applying for SFI.** The municipal course is free by law.
- **Thinking that joining an a-kassa is enough to get the benefit.** The income conditions must also be met, and you must apply.
- **Mixing up Arbetsförmedlingen and the a-kassa.** They are two things: one is the public employment agency, the other is the fund that pays the benefit.
