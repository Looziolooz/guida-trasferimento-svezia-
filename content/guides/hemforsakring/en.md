:::tip[In short]
**Home insurance (*hemförsäkring*) is not compulsory by law**, but everyone living in Sweden needs it. It is a **package of four parts**: belongings, liability, legal protection and travel cover. **If you sublet, the main tenant's policy usually does not cover you**, so take out your own. The **only** compulsory insurance is **motor third-party insurance** (*trafikförsäkring*) for a car. Konsumenternas helps you compare, and ARN handles disputes.
:::

## Do you need it if it isn't compulsory?

The law does not require it. But Konsumenternas, the independent consumer advice organisation, is clear:

> *«Du behöver en hemförsäkring, oavsett om du bor i hyresrätt, bostadsrätt eller villa.»*
> (You need home insurance whether you live in a rental flat, a tenant-owned flat or a house.)

The reason is practical. Without a policy you pay yourself after a burglary, a water leak, or damage you cause to a neighbour. For anyone newly arrived it is one of the first things to sort out, along with the rental contract (see [Finding a home](/en/guide/bostad)).

## What a hemförsäkring contains

According to Konsumenternas, every hemförsäkring is a **package policy** (*paketförsäkring*). It contains four parts, plus assault cover (*överfallsskydd*) that pays a fixed sum.

| Part | What it covers | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Property cover** (*egendomsskydd*) | Belongings (*lösöre*) you own, borrow or rent | Outside the home, items that are easy to steal are normally covered **only in case of robbery, bag-snatching or assault** |
| **Liability cover** (*ansvarsskydd*) | Damage you cause to others through negligence, **also outside your home** | Limits vary: read the terms |
| **Legal protection** (*rättsskydd*) | Legal costs in a dispute | Usually you must have held the home insurance for **2 years in a row**; the excess is usually **20–25 %** |
| **Travel cover** (*reseskydd*) | Mishaps while travelling | Usually for trips **up to 45 days** (60 in some policies) |
| **Assault cover** (*överfallsskydd*) | A fixed sum after an assault | |

:::warn[Legal protection does not apply from day one]
Legal protection usually requires **2 years in a row** with home insurance. If you switch insurer with a gap in between, you may have to start again. Ask the new insurer how it treats the years you have already built up.
:::

## What you need for your type of home

Konsumenternas sets it out like this:

- **Hyresrätt** (rental flat): you **always need your own hemförsäkring**.
- **Subletting** (*andrahandshyra*): see the warning below.
- **Bostadsrätt** (flat in a housing co-operative): the policy must be adapted. Sometimes the co-operative has a **bostadsrättstillägg**, a supplement covering your part of the property. Ask the co-operative.
- **Villa** (a house you own): you take out a combined **villa-hemförsäkring**.

:::danger[If you sublet, the main tenant's policy usually does not cover you]
Someone subletting (*andrahand*) is usually **not covered** by the main tenant's insurance. If there is a break-in or a water leak and you have no policy of your own, both your belongings and your liability are unprotected. **Take out a hemförsäkring in your own name** before you move in, and tell the insurer you live in a sublet.
:::

If you are thinking of buying, the policy is part of the cost picture: see [Buying a home in Sweden](/en/guide/kopa-bostad).

## The allrisk add-on

**Allrisk** (sometimes called *drulle*, "clumsy") covers **sudden and unforeseen damage**, for example a phone that falls on the floor. It is usually **not included** in the basic policy: it is an **add-on**. Weigh what you own against what it would cost to replace.

## Other policies, one line each

None of these is compulsory. According to Konsumenternas:

- **Accident insurance** (*olycksfallsförsäkring*): pays a sum if you are left disabled after an accident; "bra att ha" (good to have).
- **Child insurance** (*barnförsäkring*): "De flesta föräldrar väljer att teckna en barnförsäkring" (most parents choose to take out child insurance). For healthcare and children, see also [Healthcare](/en/guide/vard).
- **Life insurance** (*livförsäkring*): for a single person without children it is usually not needed.
- **Private health insurance** (*sjukvårdsförsäkring*): depends on how satisfied you are with public healthcare; often already provided by your employer. Check before paying twice.

## Car: motor third-party insurance is compulsory

If you have a car, there is **one insurance the law requires**: the **trafikförsäkring**. It is compulsory **for a vehicle in traffic, from day one**. **Halvförsäkring** and **helförsäkring** (wider cover) are **optional**.

If you bring a car from another country, the trafikförsäkring is one of the steps. Everything else (driving licence, import, winter tyres) is in [Driving licence and car](/en/guide/korkort-och-bil).

## How to compare, and where to turn

- **Compare several offers.** Excesses, limits and prices differ between insurers. Read the terms, not just the price.
- **Konsumenternas** gives independent advice: phone **0200-22 58 00**, weekdays **9–12**.
- If the insurer refuses your claim and you disagree, you can go to **ARN** (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden, the National Board for Consumer Disputes). **You must first have received a refusal from the insurer.** The fee is **150 kr**, and for insurance the **minimum value of the claim is 3,000 kr** (from 1 August 2025).

:::who[Who to ask]
**Which policy do I need?** → Konsumenternas (0200-22 58 00).
**Bostadsrätt** → the co-operative, to ask whether there is a bostadsrättstillägg.
**Car** → your insurer for the trafikförsäkring; car rules: Transportstyrelsen.
**Claim refused** → complain to the insurer first, then ARN.
:::

:::note[Prices and excesses]
We give no prices, excesses or limits: they vary a lot between insurers. Always compare the terms before you sign.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming you don't need it because it isn't compulsory.** Konsumenternas says the opposite, whatever your type of home.
- **Relying on the main tenant's policy when subletting.** It usually does not cover you.
- **Counting on legal protection from day one.** It usually takes 2 years in a row.
- **Thinking a broken phone is covered.** Accidental damage is in allrisk, which is usually an add-on.
- **Forgetting the bostadsrättstillägg** if you live in a bostadsrätt.
- **Expecting travel cover on long trips.** It usually runs up to 45 days.
- **Driving without trafikförsäkring.** It is compulsory from the first day the car is in traffic.
- **Going to ARN before the insurer has refused your claim.**
