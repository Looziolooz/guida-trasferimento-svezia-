:::tip[In short]
In Sweden you identify yourself, pay and sign with **BankID**, and BankID is issued by **your bank**. The chain is: **personnummer → accepted ID document → bank account → BankID → Swish**. If one link is missing, the rest stall. If you live in Sweden or the EEA you have a **legal right to a basic payment account**.
:::

## Why everything runs on BankID

BankID is your digital ID: an app on your phone that you use to log in, sign contracts, pay online and reach Skatteverket (the Tax Agency), Försäkringskassan, 1177 and your bank. It is not a government service, but it works almost like one.

The difficulty for newcomers is the order. Each step needs the one before:

1. **Personnummer** (the personal identity number) — arrives with your first registration in folkbokföring ([guide](/en/guide/folkbokforing)).
2. **An accepted ID document** — usually Swedish, sometimes a passport: it depends on the bank.
3. **A bank account** — the bank can only take you on if it can verify who you are.
4. **BankID** — issued by the bank to existing customers.
5. **Swish** — person-to-person payments from your phone, activated through your bank with BankID.

## Skatteverket's ID card today

Skatteverket issues a Swedish **ID-kort** (ID card) to people who are **registered in folkbokföring with a personnummer** and are at least 13 (under 18 needs a guardian's consent). It is **not a travel document** and **not for people who only have a samordningsnummer** ([guide](/en/guide/samordningsnummer)).

:::cost[Cost and timing]
**400 kr**, paid **before** your visit. You do not pay at the counter.
The card is ready in **about two weeks** and you collect it in person within two months.
:::

How it works:

- **Book an appointment** at a **servicekontor (Skatteverket service office) that issues ID cards**. Not all of them do.
- Go **in person** with a valid approved document (passport, driving licence or a Skatteverket card) **or** an **intygsgivare**.
- An **intygsgivare** is an approved witness who knows you well, for example a spouse, parent or adult child. This is the route when you have no valid document.
- Your photo is taken on the spot.

:::warn[The 2026–2027 change: read before you book]
**From 1 December 2026** the **Polismyndigheten (Swedish Police Authority)** issues the new state e-legitimation, **Sverige-id**, and residents registered in folkbokföring who are **not Swedish citizens** will be able to apply for a **special ID card from the Police**, **without a travel function**.

The government is also **proposing** to **close Skatteverket's ID card service from 1 March 2027**. This is a **proposal** (a lagrådsremiss of 3 September 2026), **not yet law**, and it may change. **According to the proposal**, cards already issued stay valid until they expire.

The cost of the Police card and of Sverige-id has not been confirmed: check the official sites. For now, people who only have a samordningsnummer are not included in the new state e-legitimation.

In practice: if you need a card now, the Skatteverket route still exists. Check for news before you book.
:::

## The bank account: it is your right

If you are **resident in Sweden or elsewhere in the EEA**, you have the right to open a **payment account with basic features** (**betalkonto**) at a Swedish bank. This comes from the Swedish payment services act.

The bank may refuse only in specific cases, for example if it cannot verify your identity, or because of anti-money-laundering rules. By law it must decide **within ten banking days** of a complete application. If it says no, it must tell you **in writing, free of charge**, and say where you can complain.

:::note[If the bank refuses: the path]
1. Ask for the refusal **in writing**.
2. Complain to the bank's **complaints officer**.
3. If that is not enough, go to the **ARN (Allmänna reklamationsnämnden, the National Board for Consumer Disputes)**.
4. Free advice: **Konsumenternas Bank- och finansbyrå, 0200-22 58 00**, weekdays 9–12.
:::

The practical obstacle is well known: many banks ask for a **personnummer** and a **Swedish ID document** before they open a full account. The right to a basic account still exists, but a bank that cannot verify you can still refuse.

### When a bank says no

- Ask **exactly which document** is required and whether a foreign passport is enough.
- Bring your original ID documents.
- Try **another bank**: internal rules differ a lot.
- If identity is the problem, consider Skatteverket's ID card (while it exists) and try again.
- Keep every written answer: you need them for a complaint.

## BankID

BankID is issued by **your bank**. In practice you need a **personnummer**, to be a customer of a bank that issues it, and an **accepted ID document**, usually Swedish. **Each bank has its own rules**: if you only have a foreign passport, **ask your bank** whether it accepts it.

### Freja eID

**Freja eID** is an e-legitimation offered as an alternative to BankID. **Check Freja's website** for which documents it accepts and whether the services you need recognise it. Not every service takes it the way it takes BankID.

### Swish

**Swish** is activated through your bank and requires **BankID on the same phone**. You use it to pay friends, flea markets and many small shops.

## While you wait

With only a **samordningsnummer**, much is harder: many banks require a personnummer, so a full account and BankID are an uphill road. Ask the bank before you spend time on it, and look at Freja. If you will stay a year or more, the right route is **folkbokföring**.

While you wait, keep your passport close and do the in-person identity check as soon as you can. The personnummer unlocks the whole chain.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking at a servicekontor that does not issue ID cards.** Check that the office does.
- **Turning up without having paid the 400 kr.** You pay in advance.
- **Treating 2027 as certain.** The closure is a proposal: follow the official news.
- **Accepting a spoken "no".** Ask for the refusal in writing; it opens the complaint route.
- **Assuming a foreign passport is enough for BankID.** It depends on the bank.
- **Looking for Swish before BankID.** The order cannot be skipped.
